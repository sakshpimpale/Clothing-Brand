Our story

Rooted in Handia.
Made for your moments.

Pradeep Vastra Bhandaar is a family-run ethnic wear store in the heart of Handia, created for women who love the ease of tradition and the joy of dressing up. Our shelves bring together graceful sarees, beautifully cut suits and fabrics worth lingering over.

Every piece is chosen for its colour, comfort and craft — so whether you are dressing for the everyday or a day you will always remember, you will find something that feels just right.

Find our physical storefront at Bajar Chok, Handia — right near the old police station and Pradeep Medical Store.
